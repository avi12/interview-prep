# The learner

Every lesson, hint and tutor reply is written for this person. To retarget the course, rewrite this file first, then follow `.claude/skills/extend-curriculum/SKILL.md`.

## Who

- A career changer preparing for **junior** interviews
- Target roles are listed in `roles.json`: backend engineer, data analyst and AI engineer, on one shared core
- Languages: TypeScript, Python and SQL

## How they learn

- They struggle to concentrate on long text. Cards stay short, every card has a picture, and a voice walks through it
- They learn best by doing: every idea ends in an exercise with real tests
- They want to know **why this way and not another way**. Every lesson names the rejected alternative and its cost, because interviews ask "why not X?"

## What the interviews look like

- A take-home plus two to three technical rounds
- Easy to medium LeetCode-style coding, SQL, a small design or case question, and behavioral questions in STAR form
- The real format is taught in `behavioral/01-the-interview-loop`
